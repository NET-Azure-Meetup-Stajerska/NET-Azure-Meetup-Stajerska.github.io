  2. 18:45 – 19:30

    Blazor Under the Hood: Components, Binding, and Re-rendering

    Žan Ivanuša

    In this session we'll look at how Blazor actually works: what a component is, how parameters flow between parent and child, how two-way binding works, and when and why components re-render.

  3. 19:30 – 20:15

    One UI to rule them all: Blazor Hybrid

    Nicola Paro

    Companies that have invested in .NET solutions for years are now facing a crucial challenge: how to evolve WinForms or WPF applications toward modern, cross-platform experiences without rewriting everything from scratch. With .NET MAUI and Blazor Hybrid, it is possible to adopt a gradual modernization approach, combining the reuse of business logic and C# components with new interfaces and cross-platform features. During this session, we will analyze architectures, patterns, and strategies for integrating Blazor Hybrid into existing applications, evaluating the advantages, limitations, and real-world use cases of a unified .NET ecosystem for web, desktop, and mobile.
